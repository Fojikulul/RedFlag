          How it works

          Four steps. About ten seconds.

          No account, no download, no waiting. The check happens on your own phone or computer.

          1. 01

            Paste

            Copy the text, email or link that worries you and paste it into the box.

          2. 02

            Scan

            RedFlag checks it against the tricks scammers use most, in four languages.

          3. 03

            Verdict

            You get a clear answer: red for likely scam, yellow for be careful, green for no signs found.

          4. 04

            Explain

            Every warning comes with a plain explanation and what to do next.

          Where to report it

          Got a scam message, or already paid? Report it. You are not alone.

          Reporting helps stop the people behind it and protects your neighbors. It is never too late, and there is nothing to be ashamed of.

          In danger right now?

          If someone is at your door demanding money, or you feel threatened, call 911.

          Call 911

          Your local sheriff or police

          Call the non-emergency number of your local sheriff’s office or police department. They can take a report and warn others.

          National Elder Fraud Hotline

          Free help for people 60 and older and their families, run by the U.S. Department of Justice.

          1-833-372-8311

          Report it online

          Tell the Federal Trade Commission. For scams that happened online, also tell the FBI.
