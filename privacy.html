Privacy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

Draft. This page describes how the site works today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet.

The short version

RedFlag checks your message with simple rules in your own browser and with an AI on our server. We do not store or sell the messages you check, and we do not ask who you are.

Messages you check

  • When you press Check it, the message is checked in two ways at once: by rules running on your phone or computer, and by an AI.
  • For the AI check, the message is sent over an encrypted connection to our server on Cloudflare, which asks Cloudflare's AI service (Workers AI) about it and returns the answer to you.
  • Our server does not save or log the message. Cloudflare processes it to produce the answer under its own terms and privacy policy, and says it does not use it to train AI models.
  • To limit misuse, our server counts how many checks come from each internet address per minute. It does not keep that address after the limit resets.
  • To show how much RedFlag is used, our server keeps a daily count of checks and how many were marked red, yellow or green. The count holds nothing about the message or about you: no text, no address, no time of day.
  • If the AI can't be reached, the rules on your device answer alone, and the page says so.
  • The message is gone when you close or reload the page.

Settings saved on your device

The site remembers your language and text size in your browser's local storage so you don't have to choose them again. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Visitor counts

We may use a privacy-friendly page counter that records page views without cookies and without identifying you. It never sees the messages you check.

Everything else

  • No accounts, no cookies for tracking, no advertising.
  • Fonts and scripts are served from this site, not from third parties.
  • The code is public, so anyone can check that this page is true.
