What RedFlag is

RedFlag is a free tool that looks for common signs of scams in messages and links. It gives general guidance only. It is not legal, financial or law enforcement advice, and it does not replace calling your bank, the police or the company a message claims to be from.

It can be wrong

RedFlag uses rules, and optionally an AI, to judge messages. Both can miss a scam or warn about a real message. A green result does not mean a message is safe. Always check through a phone number or website you already trust before you pay, share information or click a link.

Using the site

  • Use it for messages you received or want to understand.
  • Don't try to overload the AI second opinion or use it for anything other than checking messages.
