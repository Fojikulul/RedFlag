## Replies for common comments

- **"Is this safe? How do I know it's not a scam itself?"**
  Good question to ask! It's free, there's no sign-up, and it never asks for your name, number or card. What you paste is checked on your own device and isn't sent anywhere. The code is public on GitHub if anyone wants to look.

- **"It said green but I still think it's a scam."**
  Trust your gut. Green only means it didn't find the common signs. If something feels off, call the company using the number on your card or their official website, never the number in the message.

- **"I already clicked the link / paid."**
  Call your bank right away using the number on the back of your card, and report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov. If you gave a password, change it now.

## Posting tips

- Put the link in the first line. Facebook cuts long posts off after a few lines, and the link should show before "See more".
- Post Tuesday to Thursday, mid-morning. Older members tend to check in then.
- Pin a comment with the link too, so it's easy to find after the post gets busy.
- When someone shares a real scam text in the comments, thank them. Those make the best follow-up posts.
